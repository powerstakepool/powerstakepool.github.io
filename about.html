About us

A stake pool run by a dad and his daughter, Ada

I'm Keith, an Electronics and Communications Engineer from Australia, and I run the POWER Stake Pool. We've followed Cardano since late 2017, when Charles's whiteboard video first got us hooked.

Our daughter was born in December 2019. We named her Ada, after Ada Lovelace and the Cardano blockchain. As decentralisation took off, we started POWER, and today Ada and I run the pool together with a lot of love.

POWER to everyone through decentralisation, especially the kids who'll be Cardano's guardians for decades to come.

Why we do this

We believe that by the time our kids grow up, crypto will be an everyday way to pay, and that Cardano can lead the way as a truly decentralised network. Every delegator helps keep that network healthy.

Our hope is to one day hand this pool to our daughter so she can carry it forward.

What we stand for

  • Reliable block production
  • Full transparency
  • Low fees
  • Advocating for Cardano online and off
  • Inspiring parents to learn about blockchain

How we run it

  • Cloud and bare-metal servers
  • 3 relays, 2 block producers
  • 3 redundancy nodes
  • 24/7 monitoring with fail-safes

What delegators say

Kind words shared publicly on X. POWER wouldn't exist without these people.
