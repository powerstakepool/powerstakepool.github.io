How to stake in 3 steps

It takes a few minutes, and you can change pools anytime.

1

Get some ADA

Buy ADA on any exchange that lists Cardano.

2

Move it to your own wallet

Send your ADA to a Cardano wallet you control. Exchange accounts can't delegate to a pool.

3

Delegate to POWER

In your wallet's staking section, search for POWER and confirm. Rewards start arriving after a few epochs.

Staking is non-custodial. Your ADA stays in your wallet, and you can spend or move it anytime.
